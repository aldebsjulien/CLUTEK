Frequently asked questions

What does a Salesforce consulting partner do?

A Salesforce consulting partner plans, configures and rolls out Salesforce for your business, then helps you improve it over time. At Clutek that covers discovery, architecture, configuration, integrations, data migration, AI automation, training and ongoing support.

Why hire a Salesforce consultant in Montreal?

A Montreal-based consultant works in French and English, can run workshops on site, works in your time zone, and understands Quebec requirements such as Law 25 for personal information and Bill 96 for French-language customer communications.

How much does Salesforce consulting cost?

It depends on scope. A focused Sales Cloud or Service Cloud rollout for a small team costs far less than a multi-cloud program with integrations and data migration. After a free discovery call we give you a fixed scope and estimate before any work starts.

Do you only work with companies in Montreal?

No. We are based in Montreal and serve clients across Quebec, the rest of Canada and the United States, on site or remotely.
