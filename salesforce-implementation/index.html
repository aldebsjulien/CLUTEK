What a Salesforce implementation with Clutek includes

Most companies use only a fraction of what Salesforce can do. A good implementation starts from how your teams sell, serve and report, not from default settings, so people adopt the CRM and leadership gets numbers it can trust.

  • Discovery and process mapping with sales, service, marketing and finance stakeholders.
  • Solution architecture: data model, roles and sharing, automation strategy and integration design.
  • Configuration and development with Flow, Lightning Web Components and Apex where it is truly needed.
  • Data migration from spreadsheets, HubSpot, Dynamics, Zoho or a legacy Salesforce org, cleaned and de-duplicated.
  • Integrations with your ERP, billing, payments, KYC and BI tools through REST APIs or MuleSoft.
  • Training and handover in English or French, with documentation your admins can maintain.

How long a Salesforce implementation takes

A focused Sales Cloud or Service Cloud implementation for a small or mid-sized company typically goes live in 6 to 12 weeks. Multi-cloud programs, Financial Services Cloud, complex integrations or large data migrations usually take 3 to 6 months. We agree on scope, milestones and budget after a free discovery call, before any work starts.

  1. Discovery

    1 to 3 weeks of workshops and a written plan.

  2. Architecture

    Design reviewed and signed off with your team.

  3. Build

    Two-week iterations with a demo at the end of each.

  4. Go-live

    Migration, training, launch support and hypercare.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Salesforce implementation cost?

Cost depends on the clouds involved, the number of users and processes, integrations and data migration. We give a fixed scope and estimate after a free discovery call, so you know the budget before the project starts.

Can you migrate our data from another CRM?

Yes. We migrate data from spreadsheets, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, Zoho, Pipedrive and older Salesforce orgs, with cleanup, de-duplication and validation before go-live.

Do you provide support after go-live?

Yes. Every project includes launch support. After that, we offer ongoing optimization and admin support so the org keeps up with your business.
